how to play

Easy as five

  1. The ball moves by itself. Get it to the glowing middle.
  2. Tap when the gap turns gold. The ball drops in. Too soon? Wait a second.
  3. Hit the middle of the gap. You get extra points and time.
  4. Red walls bounce you back. The rings turn, so a gap will come.
  5. Beat the clock. Each middle you reach adds time and a new level. Save your art at the end.

how to play

Five calm rules

  1. Tap to turn. The light circles on its own. Tap anywhere (or press space) to reverse it.
  2. Gather the glow. Pass through soft orbs. Quick chains raise a combo and the notes climb a scale.
  3. Avoid coral. Coral shards blink before they settle. Touching one costs a breath. You have three.
  4. Perfect fit. Turn just before a coral shard, as close as you dare, for a gold snap and +25.
  5. Keep what you draw. Each orb adds a petal to your mandala and peels back hidden art. The tempo rises every 15s. At the end, mint your mandala on Sui.
